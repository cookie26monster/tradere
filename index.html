At Trādere we provide a range of services, from a single item pick up and delivery, to a full heavy load

The ONLY vehicle we provide is a medium wheelbase, high roof Citroen Berlingo. Dimensions 3m(L) x 1.5m(W) x 2m(H)

We do, however, provide driver cover for any other size vehicle up to Class 1 HGV (UK Category C+E)

We DO NOT have any other specialist qualifications or endorsements, eg. CRANE / HIAB / MOFFETT / ADR
